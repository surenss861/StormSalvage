# V4 M4: bands 3–5 (in progress, 2026-10-14)

Built on `v4-gameplay` only.
- **Unchanged:** the published game (RC8, place version 142), `main` (`0ef9465`) and the production DataStore.
- **Purchases:** all Robux purchases stay off.
- **Not done:** nothing was published or migrated.

Design rule (owner): each band adds **new gameplay**, not bigger prices on the same loop. Money is exact BigMoney/Wallet. The band table keeps an expansion path toward Qi, Dc, Vg and scientific notation (`V4-BIG-NUMBERS.md`).

## Band 3: Storm Hunter (built, verified in Studio)

| Piece | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Licence** | Storm Hunter: 4,000,000 coins, rank 4, 10 extractions, 4 industrial finds. Shown as a checklist; the refusal names each missing requirement. Tag "HUNTER". |
| **New salvage class** | Storm artifacts form in the open 30% into a storm, five per storm, typed by storm: Gale Shard (wind, 18,000), Tide Relic (flood, 26,000), Fulgurite (lightning, 36,000). They melt when the storm ends. Only Hunter-licence holders can take them. |
| **Signature event** | **The Storm's Eye.** It runs when a band 3+ player is in the server, once per storm. A calm pocket (radius 22) drifts at 5 studs/s along random legs: no wind, debris or lightning inside (floods still rise), and anyone can shelter there. After 4 s a **Storm Heart** (600,000, Legendary) forms at the centre and stays put while the Eye drifts away. |
| **Equipment** | **Storm Harness**: 5 levels, 6M → 180M, cuts storm damage and wind push by 15% per level (up to 75%). Needs the Hunter licence. |
| **Bonuses** | Band 3 event bonus 90,000 (×3 for the Press). |
| **Titles** | Storm Hunter, Eye Diver (deliver a Storm Heart), Artifact Collector. Wealth titles run to Quintillionaire. |
| **Models** | Four part-built models (`tools/asset_pipeline/build_artifacts.py`), no mesh upload needed. |
| **HUD** | Eye wall (ForceField ring; 10 panels in Reduced Effects instead of 20), calm disc, Heart beacon with distance, banner. |

### Evidence (Studio play, this session)

- **Licence:**
  - Refused with all three missing requirements named.
  - Bought through the UI: 4.5M → 500K, and the Storm Hunter title was granted.
- **Eye:**
  - It opened, drifted at 5 studs/s and spawned the Heart.
  - Shelter test in a lightning storm: 0 damage inside the Eye versus 60 outside, over 15 s each.
- **Harness:** 2 levels for 21M, WindResist 0.3.
- **Heart:**
  - Grabbed at 1,800,000 with the Press ×3.
  - Delivered for +1,800,000 with a server-wide notice.
  - Records.EyeHearts incremented and the Eye Diver title is owned. Bug fixed: the record used to be written after the coin grant, so the push and title check missed it.
- **Artifacts:**
  - Five Gale Shards, then Fulgurites, then Tide Relics formed in their storm types.
  - A Fulgurite was picked up (carried value +108,000 with the Press). The earlier failed attempts were prompt timing; I made no code change.
- **Economy sim (curve C):** first 1M at 2.2 h, band 3 entry at 6.4 h, 1B at 21.1 h, 1T at 101.8 h.
- **CI:** all specs pass.
