# V4 M4: bands 3–5 (in progress, 2026-10-14)

Built on `v4-gameplay` only.
- **Unchanged:** the published game (RC8, place version 142), `main` (`0ef9465`) and the production DataStore.
- **Purchases:** all Robux purchases stay off.
- **Not done:** nothing was published or migrated.

Design rule (owner): each band adds **new gameplay**, not bigger prices on the same loop. Money is exact BigMoney/Wallet. The band table keeps an expansion path toward Qi, Dc, Vg and scientific notation (`V4-BIG-NUMBERS.md`).

## Band 4: Heavy Salvage (built, verified in Studio)

| Piece | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Licence** | Heavy Salvage License: 1,000,000,000 coins (the first billion), Wreck Diver rank, 25 extractions, all 3 storm artifacts logged, a Storm Heart delivered. Tag "HEAVY". |
| **Signature event** | **The Armored Wreck.** It runs in the calm after a storm when a Heavy holder is in the server, for 55 s and always ending before the storm warning. An armoured bank truck lies on its side at an event site other than the one the ordinary event uses; zone salvage is kept clear within 18 studs so no "Grab" prompt steals the key. It has three locks: the rear doors, the side hatch (now facing the sky; climb the toppled crates) and the roof hatch. |
| **Cutting** | Press the lock (0.4 s), then cut while within 7 studs: 7 s per lock at stock speed, and cutters on the same lock add up. Step out to dodge and the cut **pauses**, then resumes when you return; walk past 16 studs and you've left the job. |
| **Pressure** | While anyone is cutting, a security charge fires every 3.2 s at a random cutter's feet. It uses the same telegraph, lag allowance and dodge judgement as the twister: 30 damage, and a hit knocks you off the lock until you press it again. |
| **New salvage class (Vault)** | Each cut lock throws out a Strongbox (250,000, Rare) in front of the cab. When all three locks are cut the vault opens: Bullion Bars (1,200,000, Epic), 2 plus one per cutter (at most 6), and the Crown Jewels (8,000,000, Legendary) at announced 25% odds. Vault salvage is licence-gated and sells at the Scrap Buyer. |
| **Equipment** | **Thermal Cutter**: 5 levels, 3B → 120B, cut speed ×1.3 → ×3. Needs the Heavy licence. |
| **Bonuses** | Band 4 event bonus: 1,500,000. |
| **Titles** | Heavy Hauler (licence), Safecracker (help open a vault), Crown Thief (log the Crown Jewels). |
| **Models** | Part-built in `tools/asset_pipeline/build_vault.py`: the wreck (43 parts, climbable body and crates, red lock lights that turn green when cut) plus the Strongbox, Bullion Bar and Crown Jewels. |
| **HUD** | Amber beacon over the truck and markers on uncut locks for everyone. A server progress bar on each lock. Sparks while you're in reach. Licensed players get a banner with locks cut, time left and cutting/paused state; others get only the one announcement. |

### Evidence (Studio play, this session)

- **Licence:**
  - Cutting while unlicensed was refused: "Cutting the wreck needs the Heavy Salvage License".
  - The licence purchase was refused, naming exactly the 3 missing requirements (the Heart was already met).
  - I then met them for real by picking up the last two artifacts, which granted the Artifact Collector title. The purchase gave band 4 and the Heavy Hauler title.
- **Schedule:** the wreck started on its own 10 s into Calm with a Heavy holder present. It ended at the storm warning ("vault stayed shut… hauled away").
- **Cutting under fire:**
  - Solo with the stock cutter, Lock1 took about 12 s with 4 charges dodged.
  - Lock1 and Lock3 were cut in one 55 s wreck, and time ran out at Lock2. Tool latency cost about 19 s of that window.
  - WreckLocks went up by exactly one per lock.
  - A charge hit knocked the player off the lock with a notice.
  - A bot client that sidestepped along the truck avoided most charges.
- **Climb:** a scripted walk-and-jump reached the truck top over the crates (root y 10.1, 2.7 studs from the hatch), and the hatch lock cut from there.
- **Vault (accelerated):** I set lock progress to 0.93–0.95 with the Studio-only `wreckprogress` command, then finished each lock by cutting.
  - The vault opened: "3 bullion bars spilled out" (2 + 1 cutter; no jewels on that roll).
  - VaultsOpened went to 1 and the Safecracker title was granted.
- **Sale:** a Bullion Bar picked up and sold went from exactly 200,000,125 to 203,600,125 (1.2M × Press ×3).
- **Gear:** Thermal Cutter level 1 bought for 3B. Level 2 was refused with "Need 7,500,000,000 more coins". The Gear Shop lists the Thermal Cutter with its icon.
- **Economy sim (curve C):** band 4 entry at 19.7 h, 10B at 35.6 h, 1T at 159.5 h.

### Found and fixed while playing

1. A forced wreck started after the storm warning ended immediately. Forced (QA) wrecks now ignore the storm clock, and scheduled ones refuse to start within 20 s of the storm.
2. Dodging dropped your cut, so dodging felt like failing. Fixed with the pause/resume leash.
3. Cutting was too slow solo. CutSeconds went from 9 to 7 and the charge interval from 2.6 s to 3.2 s; the sim's solo vault share is 40%.
4. Loot near the wreck stole the E key: the area is now kept clear, and strongboxes land in front of the cab.
5. Every bar was announced server-wide. Bars are now Epic (finder's card only); the Crown Jewels stay Legendary.
6. Studio QA: the asset builder ignored part collision; it now honours CanCollide/CanQuery.

**Not verified:**
- A real multi-player crew.
- The full solo vault in one natural 55 s window at stock cutter speed. The data says it's tight but possible.
- Whether it's fun: the bot runs only prove the mechanics.
- Testing during a storm kept killing the test character; scheduled wrecks never overlap a storm.

## Band 5: Storm Legend (built, verified in Studio)

| Piece | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Licence** | Storm Legend License: 20,000,000,000 coins, Storm Legend rank (the top rank), 50 extractions, 3 vaults opened, a Strongbox and a Bullion Bar logged. Tag "LEGEND". |
| **Signature event** | **The Storm Titan.** It forms 6 s into a storm when a Legend holder is present and roams town at 7 studs/s along 5 legs. Legend holders siphon it by holding the ring between 16 and 40 studs from its centre (12 s at stock Rig speed). |
| **Pressure** | Inside 16 studs it deals 18 damage/s and drains your siphon. A client pull (80 studs/s² at the inner edge) drags you in, and the Rig resists it. Telegraphed debris strikes fall where siphoners are heading (every 3 s, 20 damage). Going down loses your siphon. |
| **New salvage class (Mythic)** | A full siphon forms a Mythic Core at your feet. The kind is drawn at **published odds**: Titan Core (40M, 60%), Prism Core (100M, 30%), Singularity Core (400M, 10%). The odds appear in the formation notice and the Salvage Log, and are the actual draw weights (unit-tested at the boundaries and over 10,000 even rolls). There's a new Mythic rarity tier: colour, Log order, find card "MYTHIC!" and a server-wide call-out. |
| **Equipment** | **Containment Rig**: 5 levels, 100B → 8T, siphon speed ×1.3 → ×3, with pull resistance 1 − 1/speed set by the server (TitanResist). |
| **Bonuses** | Band 5 event bonus: 25,000,000. |
| **Titles** | Storm Legend, Titan Tamer (siphon a core), Singularity (log a Singularity Core). |
| **Models** | Three part-built cores (`build_mythic.py`). The Titan itself is drawn on the client: a stacked, widening, spinning funnel with a debris band and the magenta siphon ring plus red inner ring (fewer rings and no spin in Reduced Effects). |
| **HUD** | For Legends: a banner with distance, siphon meter, and "too close" / "siphoning" states. |

### Evidence (Studio play, this session)

- **Licence:** refused, naming all four missing requirements. For Titan QA the licence was granted with the Studio-only `grantlicense` command; the requirement logic is unit-tested and the purchase flow was shown live for bands 3 and 4.
- **First Titan:** forced with the `titan` debug command. The first tuning was far too punishing: a standing player lost about 60 HP in 8 s (pull 120, strikes every 2.4 s for 25). Retuned.
- **Siphon run:** a bot client held radius 28–30 and sidestepped strikes. Its siphon rose about 0.08/s, it took one strike hit, and a core formed.
  - The draw was a Singularity Core. The notice read "…siphoned a SINGULARITY CORE from the Storm Titan! (10% of Mythic Cores)".
  - The Titan Tamer title was granted and Records.TitanCores went to 1.
- **Sale:** a Singularity Core sold from exactly 500,000,000 to 1,700,000,000 (400M × Press ×3).
  - The core reached the bag via the debug `give` command. The player had died to lightning before the hand pickup landed; pickup uses the same licence-checked path verified for the other classes.
- **Found and fixed:**
  - The siphon meter survived a death; going down now clears it.
  - Pull tuning: 120 sucked a player straight in, 45 barely moved a standing player, so it is now 80. A human needs to judge the feel.
  - A cloud material that doesn't exist (`Smoke`), caught by selene.
- **Economy sim (curve C):** band 5 entry at 66.6 h, 1T at 114 h. 1 Qa is still beyond 200 h, which leaves room for bands 6+ on the same template toward Qi/Dc/Vg (`V4-BIG-NUMBERS.md`).

**Not verified:**
- Whether the ring is fun to hold. Only a bot held it.
- Pull feel on a phone.
- A Titan and an Eye in the same storm with several players.
