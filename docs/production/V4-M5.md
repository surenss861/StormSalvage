# V4 M5: the world (2026-10-14)

Built on `v4-gameplay` only.
- **Unchanged:** the published game (RC8, place version 142), `main` and the production DataStore.
- **Not done:** nothing was published.

## Where the space was

A raycast occupancy map of Gale Hollow (10-stud grid) showed four large empty areas inside the town walls:
- the grass either side of the Scrapyard plateau (north-west and north-east, about 110 × 120 each);
- the open grass either side of the Starlite Motel (south-west and south-east).

All four now hold districts. Every one is an event site, a loot zone and a contract district.

| District | Where | What's there | Verticality and shortcuts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Rail Yard** | NW, beside the plateau | Four tracks on ballast. Three walk-in boxcars (roofs are shelter), a flatcar with two containers (one open), a boxcar the storm tipped over, crates, buffer stops. | The **signal gantry** (13 studs, ladders at both ends and the middle) continues as a **ramp to a new gap in the plateau's west fence**: a shortcut to the sell counter. The water tower catwalk (22) and the container tops (11) are flood refuges. |
| **Flood Control Works** | NE, beside the plateau | A spillway channel between walkable banks, the sluice gate, pump house 2 (walk-in, shelter), two settling tanks, three outfall pipes to run along. | The **sluice tower** (26, ladder) has a **catwalk to a new gap in the plateau's east fence**: the second shortcut. The pump-house roof (14) is above the flood line. |
| **Gale Hollow Ballpark** | SW, by the Houses | Infield, backstop, two roofed dugouts (shelter, loot), stepped bleachers, light towers. | The scoreboard tower deck (16, ladder) is the refuge. The far run home makes it a difficulty-3 site. |
| **Pine Rest Trailer Park** | SE, by the Motel | Five walk-in trailers round a gravel loop: roofs are shelter, one roof torn off, one trailer tipped. Laundry line, mailbox row. | Trailer roofs (9.3) sit below the flood line on purpose; the Motel is next door. Difficulty-3 site. |

## Systems changed

- **Flood:** the water layer and its clearing now cover the north strip (z −335…205), not only the old 410-square town. Before this the north districts would have hurt players with no water drawn. Verified in Studio: water voxels at both north districts at peak, air on the plateau top.
- **Loot:**
  - Four new zone pools.
  - Rail Yard and Flood Control are industrial zones (Pro licence salvage).
  - The Pro licence text names them.
- **Event sites:** four new sites in Config.Events.Sites; the Pursuit, Extraction, Rush, Wreck, Eye and Titan all use them. Seen in Studio: an Extraction at Flood Control and a Wreck at the Ballpark.
- **Contracts:** Progression.Zones and ZoneOrder include the four districts. The client district labels come from MapBuilder footprints.
- **Environmental animation:** the new client `WorldMotion` module rotates parts tagged `Spin` (the valve wheel, roof vents, tank stirrers) and pulses parts tagged `Blink` (rail signals, gate lamps, floodlights). The server does no per-frame work. Only parts within 260 studs move. Reduced Effects stops the spinning and slows and softens the blinking.
