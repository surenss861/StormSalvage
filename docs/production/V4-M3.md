# V4 M3: large-scale economy and status (first slice, 2026-10-14)

> **Superseded in part:** the 1 Qa cap and the `Money` module (§1) were replaced by exact
> 303-digit money on the owner's decision. See [`V4-BIG-NUMBERS.md`](V4-BIG-NUMBERS.md).

Built on `v4-gameplay` only.
- **Unchanged:** the published game (RC8, place version 142), `main` (`0ef9465`) and the production DataStore.
- **Purchases:** all Robux purchases stay off, and there are no band-relative paid packs.
- **Not done:** nothing was published or migrated.

This slice:
1. safe coin arithmetic and the compact formatter
2. three modelled curves
3. bands 1 and 2: the Pro licence, industrial salvage, the Salvage Press, band-sized event bonuses
4. earned titles and an inspectable player card

## 1. Money: safe integers and the compact formatter (`Money.luau`)

**Range.** Coins are whole numbers from 0 to **1 Qa (10^15)**, safely below 2^53, where doubles stop being exact.

**Every grant and spend goes through `Money`:**
- sales and event bonuses (`addCoins`)
- contract rewards
- upgrades, tools, licences and nameplate finishes
- the (disabled) Robux packs

**What Money guarantees:** amounts are floored and capped; NaN, infinity, negatives and fractions are refused.

**On load, only a corrupt balance is repaired.** A valid balance is left exactly as it is.

**Display:**
- **Rounding is always down,** so 999,999 shows as "999K", never "1.00M".
- **Formats:**
  - `compact` for big labels ("1.23M", "45.6B", "1.00Qa")
  - `full` for receipts (every digit, with commas)
  - `tag` for the coin HUD (digits under a million, compact after)
- The formatter works on the integer's digits, not floats. A first version showed 999,999,999,999,999 as "1000T"; a boundary test caught it.
- **Tests:** 9 boundary groups, including every power of ten and one below it.

## 2. Progression curves (simulation: `tools/econ/sim.luau`)

**The model.** A deterministic engaged player:
- **Calibrated:** trips of `capacity` items, matching V3's measured ~84 coins/min early.
- **Events:** one per 2.45-min storm cycle, half of them extracted.
- **Spending:** greedy, on income only (no cosmetics, so it's the *fastest* realistic case).

**Inputs.** Bands 1–2 read the real `Bands.luau` table. Bands 3–5 extrapolate the same structure. Output: `evidence/m3-economy-sim.txt`.

| Curve | 1K | 10K | 100K | **1M** | 10M | 1B | 1T | Band 2 entry |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A: original draft (licence 75K, items ~10×) | 6 min | 57 min | 7.6 h | **10.9 h** | 14.3 h | 39.9 h | not within 200 h | 7.3 h |
| B: faster (licence 25K, items ~40×) | 6 min | 57 min | 3.0 h | **5.6 h** | 10.6 h | 30.8 h | 82.6 h | 2.7 h |
| **C: early fortune (built)** | 6 min | 51 min | 1.1 h | **2.2 h** | 7.3 h | 14.4 h | 66 h | **51 min** |

**C is what's implemented.**
- **The early fortune:** the Pro licence arrives within the first hour, with a ~30× income jump (band 2: about 5,300/min at entry, about 33,600/min with the full Press). So the first million lands around **2.2 h**, not 10 h.
- **Later goals stay long:** billions (about 14 h), trillions (about 66 h) and later licences remain long-term goals. Bands 3–5 also gate on accomplishments (modelled at 10, 30 and 70 h).

**These are model estimates for an efficient player.** Real players will be slower: cosmetics, deaths, missed events. They need checking against real sessions; the sim is a tuning tool, not evidence.

## 3. Bands 1–2 (built, `Bands.luau`)

**Band 1, Town:** today's game, unchanged. Same salvage, gear and events; extraction bonus 150 / 120; Golden Core 600.

**Band 2, Pro**, entered with the **Pro Salvage License** (Gear Shop › Tools):
- **5,000 coins AND a Salvager rank AND 3 successful extractions.** Coins can't buy the accomplishments; the server checks all three.
- **Industrial salvage**, a new class of things to chase:

  | Item | Rarity | Value |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Copper Wire Spool | Uncommon | 400 |
  | Hydraulic Pump | Rare | 1,200 |
  | Diesel Generator | Epic | 3,000 |
  | Turbine Rotor | Legendary | 9,000 |

  - **Where:** up to 3 per Warehouse, Fuel & Go and Supermarket yard, under their own cap, so they never crowd out ordinary salvage.
  - **Who:** everyone sees it; only licence holders can take it (server check).
  - **Detector:** never pinged.
  - **Models:** four distinct part-built machines; no uploads.
- **The Salvage Press:** a sale multiplier, ×1.5 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 6 / 8, costing 20K / 60K / 150K / 400K / 1M / 2.5M. Needs the licence (server check).
- **Band-sized extraction bonuses:** Pursuit 6,000, Extraction 5,000, Golden ×3 (18,000). The bigger number is tied to the meaningful activity (the chase), not to inflated prices.

## 4. Status: titles and the player card

**Nine earned titles** (`Titles.luau`, server-granted only):

| Category | Titles |
| --- | --- |
| Pursuit (skill and spectacle) | Stormbreaker, Quick Hands (under 20 s), Untouchable (3 clean in a row), Daredevil (hardest site), Jackpot Hunter (Golden extracted) |
| Discovery | Collector (all 12 town kinds), Turbine Hunter |
| Career | Professional (the licence) |
| Wealth | First Fortune (1M lifetime) |

Only one of the nine is about wealth.

**How titles work:**
- **Granting:** checked before every state push, so anything that changes records, discoveries, earnings or licences is caught. Each grant gets its own "TITLE EARNED" card.
- **Showing:** chosen on the Jobs board's new **TITLES** tab. The shown title replaces the rank on the nameplate, in gold.

**The player card:** tap or click another player, or **MY CARD**.
- **Shows:** rank, band, shown title, lifetime earned (compact plus exact), extractions, best time, best clean streak, golden cores out, hardest site, Salvage Log and titles.
- **Never shows:** the coin balance, or anything a client sent.

**Ranks and nameplate finishes are unchanged.**

## 6. Old saves, monetary scaling and rollback (assessment, before any migration)

**No migration was done, and none is needed for this slice.** Everything is additive and reconciled on load:
- `Licenses = {}`
- `Titles = { Owned = {} }`
- `Upgrades.Press = 0`
- `Records.GoldenExtractions = 0`

**Same namespace.** Nothing is renamed, reset or removed. Balances are untouched; only a corrupt one would be repaired.

**Rollback tested: RC9 code on an M3 profile.** Run in Studio on the test profile, using the exact `8ccc3ff` scripts:
- It loaded with no errors: 38,900 coins shown, Press level kept, and a Press purchase attempt ignored.
- After RC9 saved it, the stored profile still held `Licenses.Pro`, all 7 titles (Daredevil shown), `Press = 1` and `GoldenExtractions = 1`.
- Rolling forward again restores everything.

**Hazards:**
1. **Unspendable balances on rollback.** A band-2 player rolled back to RC9 or RC8 keeps their coins (possibly millions) but loses the licence, industrial salvage and the Press. The data comes back on roll-forward, and the balances could buy every band-1 item at once.
   - **That's harmless to data but changes the experience.** It's the same hazard as noted in V4-ECONOMY §8.
   - **Mitigation:** keep rollback windows short, and decide the **V4 save namespace question (an owner gate)** before public release.
2. **Inflation pressure.** Band-2 incomes (5K–34K/min) dwarf band-1 prices. That's intended: band 1 becomes something you outgrow. Sinks for band 2 are the Press (about 4.1M total) and future cosmetics.
   - Bands 3+ must add sinks before they ship, or band-2 balances will pile up.
   - The Robux catalogue must not be priced against band-1 numbers. Packs stay off; band-relative packs need explicit approval.
3. **Ceiling.** 1 Qa is the hard cap (tested). If the design ever needs more, it needs a different number type and a real migration: a future owner decision.

## 7. The lag-protection question from the M2 review

**The concern:** 0.35 s grace + up to 0.25 s ping allowance is a protection window.

**What Studio showed (M2 part 2):**
- **No added lag:** a dodge pressed after the ring visibly closes is **hit** (1.36 s and 1.40 s presses: hit). The window matches the player's own view; it doesn't extend it.
- **The allowance:** it only grows with the player's measured ping, capped at 0.25 s.
- **Session logging:** the session report records the largest allowance applied (`StrikeLagAllowanceMs`).

**For the real-server test:**
- **Inputs:** the logged allowances, plus two players watching each other.
- **Check:** whether anyone avoids a hit after the warning ended on the *other* player's screen.
- **If so:** shrink the cap. No change was made without that evidence.

## 8. Remaining launch risks

- **Fun and pacing:** whether band 2 feels like a reward and the chase stays thrilling with bigger numbers. Needs real players.
- **Real-time milestone times:** only simulated so far; measure with real sessions.
- **Controls:** phone and controller for the dodge, touch for the player card (tap on a character). Unverified on devices.
- **Real multiplayer:** two players inspecting each other, seeing titles on nameplates, and fairness under real ping.
- **Bands 3–5:** designed only. They need new content and sinks before the numbers go past band 2.
- **Owner gates:** the save namespace decision, Robux catalogue redesign and activation, and public release.
- **Animations:** still waiting on the owner's uploads (M2).
